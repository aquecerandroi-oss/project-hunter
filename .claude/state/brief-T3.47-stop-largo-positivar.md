## Why this lever and not position size (state it in the report, in Portuguese)
Position size multiplies the result in R$; it never changes the sign. What turns an entry from negative to positive is the toll per operation: `custo_R = 0,0020 / (stop_atr × ATR%)` (KB-0076, T3.40). A wider stop in ATR is exactly "mais arriscado por entrada" (more distance to the stop) **and** less toll per R — the two things Everton asked for, without touching the risk limits (which are his: `risk_per_trade_pct 0,25 %` stays; T3.48 documents the size question separately).
