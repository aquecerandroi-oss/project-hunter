## The bug (code-reviewer, 2026-09-08, confirmed in production)
`recovery.py:147` reads `market_earliest` once per cycle; the history loop (`recovery.py:243-271`) and the checks in `recovery_drain.py:109` and `:260` compare `gap.gap_end < earliest_known` against that stale value for every gap of the same market in the same cycle. When `interleave` grants a market more than one slot in a cycle (it does whenever few markets compete — `test_a_market_with_a_small_backlog_is_never_starved_by_a_bigger_one` gives one market 4 of 6), chunk 1 (`gap_end == T0`) is recovered and pushes the real minimum to `T0 − 4h`; chunk 2 (`gap_end == T0 − 4h`) is compared against the stale `T0`, classified `unrecoverable/before_listing`, and by design never reopened. **On the VPS at 14:00Z: BTCUSDT 5 unrecoverable, UNIUSDT 5 unrecoverable** — both listed years ago; those are legitimate August windows lost. The VPS market-workers were rolled back to image `385dac6` at ~14:10Z until this lands.
