## Why (measured, `t30-proof.md` §1 and `notes-T3.0c.md` §1)
With 200 perpetuals on the same event loop, enabling the spot collector in shard 0 made the perpetual socket miss its keepalive: code 1011, 8 reconnects in 6 minutes, 0 candles persisted. So `MARKET_SPOT_ENABLED` defaults to `false`, and the paper wallet has no spot last trade, no spot book and no spot minute volume in the hot state — the admission returns `unavailable` and refuses every entry. Everton has decided to enable the paper flow (2026-09-08, "bora ativar tudo"); the safe way to enable spot is a **dedicated process**, not a flag on a perpetual shard.
