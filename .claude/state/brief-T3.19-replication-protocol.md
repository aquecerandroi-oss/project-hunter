## The protocol to freeze (write it as `docs/plans/REPLICATION.md`, Portuguese, numbered, with every threshold named)
A version becomes **promissora** when the T3.18 scoreboard says `validada` for the first time (mature: ≥ 100 evaluable outcomes AND ≥ 30 distinct days; expectancy_r > 0; profit_factor > 1). Promising is not real. Real requires **four independent repetitions** to agree:
1. **Fora da amostra (tempo)**: from the instant the version became promising (`promising_at`, recorded), only outcomes with `decision_at > promising_at` count for the out-of-sample block; it must reach its own maturity (≥ 50 outcomes AND ≥ 15 days — half the original rule, state why) with expectancy_r > 0.
2. **Irmãs (parâmetros)**: N = 10 sibling versions, `research_only`, `status = active`, derived from the promising version by jittering every numeric parameter independently by a uniform ±15 % (integers rounded, bounds respected by the parameter schema; seeded RNG, seed recorded), each a real `strategy_versions` row with its own `code_ref` (same module), cohort `replication:<parent_version_id>:<k>`; **at least 7 of 10** must be mature-at-half-rule with expectancy_r > 0. Siblings are created by the new script `infra/scripts/replicate_strategy_version.py <key> <version> --siblings 10 --seed <n> [--dry-run]` (owner connection, audited in `system_events` like the activation script; never touches the parent; refuses if the parent is not `validada` on the scoreboard rule, unless `--force-research` with a reason — for Everton's manual experiments only).
3. **Metades de mercado**: split the parent's distinct markets into two halves by a deterministic hash; both halves must have expectancy_r > 0 over the mature population. Pure computation on existing outcomes, no new cohort.
4. **Bootstrap**: 1 000 resamples with replacement of the parent's evaluable net-R outcomes (seeded); the 95 % interval of the mean must exclude 0. Also report the p-value of the sign test. Pure function in `packages/indicators/hunter_indicators/replication/` with property tests (a fair coin never passes; a shifted distribution passes; N < 30 refuses with a reason).
Verdict `real` only when 1–4 pass; `promissora, replicando` while any block is immature; `refutada` when a mature block fails (siblings majority negative, out-of-sample negative, a market half negative, or the interval crosses 0). Refuted stays research; nothing is activated or deprecated automatically — the script and the scoreboard only **say**; Everton decides.
