"vamos ficar simulando então a operação simulada tem que simular 500mil por dia … 500mil é 500mil validação". Read as: **five hundred thousand simulated operations per day** as validation mass, not money. ## Deliver
1. **Replay engine as a job** (`hunter_strategy_worker/replay/engine.py` + a runner `python -m hunter_strategy_worker.replay.run --version <id> --from <date> --to <date> --markets all|<list> --cohort replay:<uuid>`): evaluates the version bar by bar over persisted `candles` (1m aggregated to the strategy timeframe, `is_final` only, context cut at `source_bar_close` exactly as the live path — reuse the live evaluation function, never a second implementation), emits signals into `agent_signals`/`signal_outcomes` under a **replay cohort** (0012 grammar; T3.19c), resolves outcomes with the same walker and the same assumed costs, writes nothing to the outbox that the bridge consumes (replay cohorts are refused there anyway — prove it with a test), and records a `replay_runs` ledger row (id, version, window, markets, started/finished, signals, outcomes, bars evaluated, seconds) — if that table needs a migration, write the brief for `database-architect` instead of the migration.
2. **Throughput**: measure bars/second on the local stack for one version × one market × 31 days, then scale: parallel workers per market (process pool, not threads), a `REPLAY_BUDGET` config (max CPU share, max concurrent runs, pause when the live scanner's readiness degrades), and a queue so the plantão can enqueue "replicate version X" and the engine drains it. Target: **≥ 500 000 simulated operations/day** on the VPS budget without touching the live path's latency — say in numbers what one day of replay produces (versions × markets × days × signals/day) and what it costs (CPU-hours, rows, disk).
3. **No look-ahead, provably**: property tests that a replayed decision at bar `t` never reads a bar `> t`, that assumed costs equal the live ones, that replay and live agree on the same window (run the live cohort's own window through the replay and compare outcomes bar by bar — the "same code, same answer" test).
4. **Plantão integration**: the scoreboard (T3.18) reads replay cohorts separately from live ones (label "replay: N operações sobre <janela>"), never merging them into the live maturity rule; the replication protocol's sibling block may use replay to reach maturity faster — say explicitly in `docs/plans/REPLICATION.md` §3 what replay may and may not count for (Everton's decision if it changes the rule: propose, do not decide).
5. **Docs**: `docs/PIPELINE.md` (replay engine section), `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` (how to run and budget it on the VPS), `docs/plans/REPLICATION.md` §9 cost update.
