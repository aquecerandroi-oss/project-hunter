5. Related work
That execution access shapes realized outcomes and adverse selection is established in equities market
microstructure, where differential access to venues affects execution outcomes (Brugler and Comerton-
Forde, 2025); we neither claim nor rely on the novelty of that general point. Related automated-market-
makertheoryformalizesadverse-selectioncoststhroughloss-versus-rebalancing,showinghowstaleAMM
prices can transfer value to better-informed arbitrageurs (Milionis et al., 2022); this general mechanism is
established prior art we invoke, not our contribution. On-chain, the latency race at token launch is widely
known, and adjacent empirical work characterizes coordinated sniper cohorts (Kamat, 2026a, whose es-
timated SOL-inflow lift is not distinguishable from zero — consistent with our refusal of any capture or
causalclaim),filterperformanceonrejectedtokens(Kamat,2026b),andgraduationdynamics(Marinoet
al.,2026;Kamat,2026c). Eachaddressesone component—accesseffects, adverseselection,or bonding-
curve behavior — in an adjacent setting; we did not find a study that joins them into a single realizability
decomposition. Our contribution is that join — price-space opportunity, access probability, reached-set
composition,andarealizable-returnbound—measuredwheretheaccesslegisexactandthereturnlega
conditional optimistic bound. We are not aware of prior empirical work documenting this decomposition
inabonding-curvegraduationmarket,andweclaimnomore.
